How it works

What your 3-day close looks like

Set up once in an afternoon. After that, every month follows the same three steps.

  1. Connect your accounts

    Link your banks, cards and ERP with read-only access. Closebook pulls in transactions every night, so nothing piles up for month end.

    ~40 min setup · 11,000+ banks supported

  2. Review the exceptions

    Closebook matches the routine items and sends you a short queue of what's left, with a suggested fix for each one.

    Typical queue: 20–60 items

  3. Lock the period

    Approve the reconciliations, post adjusting entries back to your ERP and export an audit-ready close package in one click.

    Sign-off trail saved automatically

FAQ

Questions controllers ask before they start

How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their banks and ERP in about 40 minutes. Closebook then imports 12 months of history to learn your matching patterns, which runs overnight. You can work your current month the next morning.

Will this replace our ERP or our accountants?

No. Closebook sits beside your ERP and does the matching work. Your team keeps every decision that needs judgment, and the results post back to the ERP you already use.

Is our financial data secure?

Bank connections are read-only. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and Closebook is SOC 2 Type II certified. We can share our report under NDA during your trial.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is charged automatically, because we never ask for a card up front. Pick a plan to keep going, or export your reconciliations and leave. Your data is deleted 30 days later.

Can we run it alongside our current process for a month?

Yes, and we recommend it. Run your normal close in parallel and compare the two. Most teams switch fully after their first month.
